## 👋 About me

AI Engineer who builds production LLM agents end to end: tool calling, retrieval, evaluation and tracing, and the backend infrastructure that keeps them reliable under real traffic. Background in computer vision; B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence Engineering from Mansoura University, first in class.

## 🚀 What I've built

- **[Hyperloop](https://hyperloop.so)**: designed and built the production AI agent answering customers for 20 e-commerce brands across 5 channels at ~3.3k AI turns a day: Gemini tool calling, RAG over product catalogues and FAQs, plus a self-learning loop from the human team's replies.
- **MatchedBy**: built an LLM analytics assistant that lets ops teams query call data in plain English, typed or by live voice dictation, with tool calling and streamed answers.
- **Eyedentify**: founding engineer at an AUC V-Lab startup (Cycle 23); led the ML and backend of its licence-plate recognition product, fine-tuning YOLO on a ~10k-image dataset of Egyptian plates (0.91 mAP50-95).

<sub>That work lives in private company repositories. My public projects are below.</sub>
